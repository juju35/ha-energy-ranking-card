# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- First release: `energy-ranking-card` and `energy-ranking-mini-card`. The devices that use the most energy today, this week or this month, ranked with a bar, their kWh, their cost and their trend against the same stretch of the period before; Day / Week / Month tabs; an *Other and unmeasured* line (the grid import minus the devices shown). Devices, grid import and price from the Energy dashboard by default, or sensors, a total sensor, a fixed price or a price entity of the config; with a price entity that has statistics, each hour at its own price (€/MWh and cents converted). Statistics loaded once the card is on screen. An illustrated header with bars in the status colour and a flickering bolt; the mini card shows the device in the lead, the total and the trend on two lines.
